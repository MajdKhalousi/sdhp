# Vision

**Last updated:** Phase 0B

## Purpose

This document states what Elaji Health is trying to become and why. It is the top of the documentation hierarchy — every product, architecture, and engineering decision should be traceable back to this vision, or to the approved decisions in [Decisions/](Decisions/).

## Vision Statement

Elaji Health is building a complete **Clinic Operating System** for small and medium private clinics — one platform that covers administration, medical records, clinical workflows, billing, HR, and reporting, instead of clinics stitching together separate tools for each.

The first priority is making this system genuinely good for **one real clinic running it every day**, not for impressing many clinics at once. Breadth and scale come after depth and reliability are proven with a real customer.

The architecture is deliberately built so that, over time, it can grow into a broader health platform — one where a patient's medical identity can be recognized across multiple subscribed clinics, with the patient's consent, rather than every clinic holding a totally isolated copy of "their own" patient. This is a long-term direction, not a near-term commitment — see [Product-Scope.md](Product-Scope.md) for what is actually in scope today versus later.

## Why This Matters

Small and medium private clinics are usually underserved by software: too small for enterprise hospital systems, too operationally complex for generic scheduling apps. Elaji Health's bet is that a clinic-sized, clinic-shaped product — built clinic-first rather than hospital-first or ministry-first — is the right starting point, with room to grow without a rewrite.

# Decision 002 — First Business Goal

**Status:** Approved
**Last updated:** Phase 0B

## Purpose

States what "success" means right now, so work is prioritized against a real near-term goal instead of a hypothetical future scale.

## Decision

The first commercial goal is to prepare the system for **one real clinic pilot/customer**, before scaling to many customers.

## Why This Matters

It is easy to over-build for imagined future customers (multi-tenant edge cases, enterprise features, scale concerns) before a single real clinic has actually used the product daily. This decision deliberately orders the work: prove the product works for one real, paying clinic first; generalize and scale only after that's true. This is consistent with [Decision-005](Decision-005-Product-Inventory-First.md) — understand and stabilize what exists before adding more.

# Decision 001 — Target Customer

**Status:** Approved
**Last updated:** Phase 0B

## Purpose

Defines who Elaji Health is being built for first, so product and engineering decisions have a concrete customer to be judged against instead of an abstract "any healthcare provider."

## Decision

Elaji Health's first target customer is a **private medium-sized clinic** —

- **not** a hospital
- **not** a ministry of health
- **not** a national health network

## Why This Matters

A medium-sized private clinic has a specific shape: one or a few branches, a handful of doctors, a front desk, an in-house billing/cashier function, and no dedicated IT department. Features, defaults, and UX should be judged against this customer, not against the needs of a 500-bed hospital or a government health authority — those are explicitly different products with different scale, compliance, and integration requirements.
